Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information JJTECHNONIC Publisher (the "Publisher") collects, how it uses TikTok data and permissions, how that data is protected, and how you can disconnect your account and have your data deleted. The Publisher is operated by JJTECHNONIC ("we", "us"), Philippines, who is responsible for this data.

1. Summary

  • The Publisher uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API (Upload), with two permissions: user.info.basic and video.upload.
  • It uses them only to identify your connected account and to upload videos you have approved to your TikTok inbox as drafts. You publish every post yourself in the TikTok app.
  • Your data is stored on the operator's own computer, not in a cloud database run by us.
  • We do not sell, rent or share TikTok data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.
  • You can disconnect at any time, and your stored tokens and account data are then deleted.

3. Information you provide

  • Videos you approve for upload. These are your files, kept in the Office's local media storage on the operator's computer.
  • Suggested text such as captions, hashtags and calls to action. These stay in the Office for you to copy; they are not sent to TikTok by the Publisher.
  • Messages you send to us by email, for example a privacy or deletion request.

4. How we use TikTok permissions

user.info.basic (Login Kit)

Used only to identify the connected account and display its name and avatar inside the Publisher.

video.upload (Content Posting API, Upload)

Used only when you approve a specific finished video for upload. The Publisher then uploads that video file to your TikTok inbox as a draft and checks its upload status. It does not publish anything; you finish and publish the draft yourself in the TikTok app. The Publisher never uploads automatically, on a schedule, or in the background without your approval.

We do not use TikTok data for any other purpose, including advertising, profiling, selling, or training machine-learning or AI models.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade any data. TikTok data is not shared with any third party. Data is exchanged only:

  • with TikTok, when the Publisher calls TikTok's API to carry out an action you requested; and
  • with authorities, only if required by law, and then only the minimum required.

6. Where data is stored and how it is protected

  • The Publisher runs on the operator's own computer. Account details, tokens and upload records are stored there, not in a cloud database operated by us.
  • Access tokens, refresh tokens and the app's client secret are stored outside the Office's code and data files, are never shown on screen or written to logs, and are not committed to any code repository.
  • All communication with TikTok uses HTTPS.
  • Access to the computer and the Office is limited to the operator and people the operator authorizes.
  • We request only the two permissions listed above.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. If we learn of a security incident affecting your data, we will notify you and, where required, the Philippine National Privacy Commission.

8. Disconnecting your account and deleting your data

You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time, in any of these ways:

  • In the Publisher: choose Disconnect for the connected account. The Publisher asks TikTok to revoke its access, then deletes the stored tokens, account identifiers, display name, avatar link, granted scopes and upload records for that account.
  • In the TikTok app: remove the Publisher from the apps connected to your account in your TikTok settings. The Publisher's tokens then stop working, and we delete the stored data the next time the Publisher detects the revoked access, or when you ask us.
  • By email: write to paemalcaz03@gmail.com. We will disconnect the account and delete its stored data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Disconnecting does not delete drafts or posts already in your TikTok account. You can manage or delete those in the TikTok app.

9. Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and other applicable laws, you may have the right to be informed, to access your data, to object, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have your data erased or blocked, to data portability, and to claim damages. To use any of these rights, email paemalcaz03@gmail.com. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines.

10. Children

The Publisher is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly connect accounts belonging to minors. If you believe a minor's account has been connected, contact us and we will disconnect it and delete its data.

11. Cookies and tracking

This website and the Publisher do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or tracking pixels.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update the "Last updated" date when this policy changes. If we ever plan to collect new TikTok data or request a new permission, we will update this policy before doing so, and TikTok will ask you to approve the new permission.

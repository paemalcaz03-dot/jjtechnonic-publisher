Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Current status: in development. JJTECHNONIC Publisher has not been built yet. As of the date above, it cannot connect to TikTok, and it collects, receives and stores no TikTok data. Section 1 describes what is collected today. Sections marked Planned describe how the Publisher is designed to handle data once it is built. We will update this policy, and the "Last updated" date, before any of those features go live.

This Privacy Policy explains what information JJTECHNONIC Publisher (the "Publisher") collects now, how it is designed to use TikTok data and permissions once built, how that data is planned to be protected, and how disconnection and deletion are planned to work. The Publisher is operated by JJTECHNONIC ("we", "us"), Philippines, who is responsible for this data.

1. What we collect today

  • TikTok data: none. No TikTok account can be connected yet, so we receive no TikTok account information, tokens or content.
  • Emails you send us: if you write to paemalcaz03@gmail.com, we receive your email address and message, and use them only to reply.
  • This website: it does not use cookies, analytics trackers, or tracking pixels. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub, as the hosting provider, may process technical data such as visitors' IP addresses to deliver and secure the site, under GitHub's own privacy policy.

2. Planned summary

  • The Publisher is designed to use TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API (Upload), with two permissions: user.info.basic and video.upload.
  • It is designed to use them only to identify your connected account and to upload videos you have approved to your TikTok inbox as drafts. You will publish every post yourself, manually, in the TikTok app.
  • Its data is designed to be stored on the operator's own computer, not in a cloud database run by us.
  • We will not sell, rent or share TikTok data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.
  • You will be able to disconnect at any time, and the Publisher is designed to delete your stored tokens and account data when you do.

4. Information you will provide (Planned)

  • Videos you approve for upload. These are your files, intended to be kept in the Office's local media storage on the operator's computer.
  • Suggested text such as captions, hashtags and calls to action. These are intended to stay in the Office for you to copy; the Publisher is not designed to send them to TikTok.

5. How TikTok permissions will be used (Planned)

user.info.basic (Login Kit)

To be used only to identify the connected account and display its name and avatar inside the Publisher.

video.upload (Content Posting API, Upload)

To be used only when you approve a specific finished video for upload. The Publisher is designed to then upload that video file to your TikTok inbox as a draft and check its upload status. It is not designed to publish anything: you will finish and publish the draft yourself in the TikTok app. The Publisher is not designed to upload automatically, on a schedule, or in the background without your approval.

We will not use TikTok data for any other purpose, including advertising, profiling, selling, or training machine-learning or AI models.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade any data, now or in the future. Once the Publisher is built, TikTok data will not be shared with any third party. Data will be exchanged only:

  • with TikTok, when the Publisher calls TikTok's API to carry out an action you requested; and
  • with authorities, only if required by law, and then only the minimum required.

7. Planned storage and security measures (Planned)

These measures are design requirements for the Publisher. They are not in place yet, because the Publisher does not exist yet and holds no data. The Publisher is being designed so that:

  • it runs on the operator's own computer, with account details, tokens and upload records stored there, not in a cloud database operated by us;
  • access tokens, refresh tokens and the app's client secret are stored outside the Office's code and data files, are not shown on screen or written to logs, and are not committed to any code repository;
  • all communication with TikTok uses HTTPS;
  • access to the computer and the Office is limited to the operator and people the operator authorizes; and
  • it requests only the two permissions listed above.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. If we learn of a security incident affecting your data, we will notify you and, where required, the Philippine National Privacy Commission.

9. Disconnecting your account and deleting your data

Available now: no account can be connected and we hold no TikTok data, so there is nothing to disconnect or delete. You can still email paemalcaz03@gmail.com with any privacy question or request, and we will reply within 30 days.

Planned: once account connection is available, you will be able to disconnect your TikTok account at any time, in any of these ways:

  • In the Publisher: a Disconnect option, designed to ask TikTok to revoke the Publisher's access and then delete the stored tokens, account identifiers, display name, avatar link, granted scopes and upload records for that account.
  • In the TikTok app: removing the Publisher from the apps connected to your account in your TikTok settings. The Publisher's tokens will then stop working. The Publisher is designed to delete the stored data when it detects the revoked access; you can also ask us to delete it.
  • By email: writing to paemalcaz03@gmail.com. We will disconnect the account and delete its stored data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Disconnecting will not delete drafts or posts already in your TikTok account. You manage or delete those in the TikTok app.

Planned: the Publisher is being designed as the publishing component of the JJTECHNONIC Office, a video production workspace that runs on the operator's own computer. Once built, it is intended to let the owner of a TikTok account:

4. Your approval before every upload (Planned)

The Publisher is designed to upload a video only after you approve that specific video. Before you approve, the JJTECHNONIC Office is intended to show you the video, its quality-check results and its suggested caption, hashtags, call to action and cover. After you approve, the Publisher is intended to upload the video file only. TikTok does not accept a caption or cover with a draft upload, so you will add those yourself in the TikTok app. The Publisher is intended to show the upload's status and tell you to finish the post from your TikTok inbox.

Draft uploads will be subject to TikTok's own limits, including a limit on how many drafts can be pending at the same time.

What it is planned to be

JJTECHNONIC Publisher is being designed as the publishing component of the JJTECHNONIC Office, a video production workspace that runs on the operator's own computer. Once built, when a video is finished and the account owner approves it in the Office, the Publisher is intended to upload it to the owner's TikTok account as a draft. The owner would then open the TikTok app, finish the post (caption, cover, music and settings) and decide whether to publish it manually. The Publisher is designed never to publish anything itself.

Planned workflow

  • Connect (Planned). The account owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and approves the requested permissions on TikTok's own consent screen.
  • See the connected account (Planned). The Publisher shows the connected account's display name and avatar, so the owner can confirm the right account is in use.
  • Review and approve (Planned). In the Office, the owner previews the finished video and its suggested caption, hashtags and cover, and approves that specific upload. Nothing is to be uploaded without this approval.
  • Upload as a draft only (Planned). The Publisher sends the approved video to the owner's TikTok inbox. TikTok notifies the owner, who finishes and publishes the post manually in the TikTok app.
  • Disconnect at any time (Planned). The owner can disconnect the account; the Publisher is designed to then revoke its access and delete its stored tokens and account data.

What it is designed not to do

  • It is not designed to publish posts. Every post will be finished and published by the account owner in the TikTok app.
  • It is not designed to upload automatically, on a schedule, or in the background without the owner's approval.
  • It is not designed to read your existing TikTok videos, followers, comments, messages or analytics.
  • It will not show ads, sell data, or share TikTok data with third parties.

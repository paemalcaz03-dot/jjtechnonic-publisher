JJTECHNONIC Publisher

A publishing tool operated by JJTECHNONIC, Philippines.

JJTECHNONIC Publisher is the publishing component of the JJTECHNONIC Office, a video production workspace that runs on the operator's own computer. When a video is finished and the account owner approves it in the Office, the Publisher uploads it to the owner's TikTok account as a draft. The owner then opens the TikTok app, finishes the post (caption, cover, music and settings) and decides whether to publish it. The Publisher never publishes anything itself.

How it works

  • Connect. The account owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and approves the requested permissions on TikTok's own consent screen.
  • See the connected account. The Publisher shows the connected account's display name and avatar, so the owner can confirm the right account is in use.
  • Review and approve. In the Office, the owner previews the finished video and its suggested caption, hashtags and cover, and approves that specific upload. Nothing is uploaded without this approval.
  • Upload as a draft. The Publisher sends the approved video to the owner's TikTok inbox. TikTok notifies the owner, who finishes and publishes the post in the TikTok app.
  • Disconnect at any time. The owner can disconnect the account, which revokes the Publisher's access and deletes its stored tokens and account data.

What it does not do

  • It does not publish posts. Every post is finished and published by the account owner in the TikTok app.
  • It does not upload automatically, on a schedule, or in the background without the owner's approval.
  • It does not read your existing TikTok videos, followers, comments, messages or analytics.
  • It does not show ads, sell data, or share TikTok data with third parties.
